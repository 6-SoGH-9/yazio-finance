Add a year from the PDF

Drop the annual report PDFs (HGB, as printed in the Unternehmensregister). The page reads the balance sheet and income statement, solves the signs against the printed subtotals, compares with data/years.js, runs the model checks and gives you the year entry and an updated years.js. The PDF never leaves your browser.
